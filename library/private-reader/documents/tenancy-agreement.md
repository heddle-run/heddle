# Assured shorthold tenancy agreement

**Property:** Flat 4, 118 Cranmer Road, Bristol BS6 5PT
**Landlord:** Marlowe Property Holdings Ltd
**Tenant:** A. Okonjo
**Term:** 12 months from 1 March 2026

## 1. Rent

1.1 The rent is £1,340 per calendar month, payable in advance on the first
working day of each month by standing order.

1.2 Rent unpaid 14 days after the due date carries interest at 3% above the
Bank of England base rate, calculated daily from the due date and not from the
end of the 14 days.

1.3 The rent may be reviewed once, on the anniversary of the term, and any
increase is capped at the annual CPI figure published for the preceding
December plus one percentage point.

## 2. Deposit

2.1 The deposit is £1,546, protected with the Deposit Protection Service under
scheme reference DPS-88213-Q.

2.2 The deposit is returned within 10 days of the end of the tenancy, less any
deduction the landlord itemises in writing. Cleaning may only be deducted where
the property is left less clean than recorded in the inventory at check-in, and
not to a standard higher than that record.

## 3. Ending the tenancy

3.1 The tenant may end this agreement after the sixth month by giving two
months' notice in writing, expiring on the last day of a rental period. This is
a break clause and cannot be exercised before month six.

3.2 The landlord may not serve notice under section 21 during the fixed term.

3.3 Leaving before the break clause is available makes the tenant liable for
rent to the end of the fixed term, or until a replacement tenant is found and
begins paying, whichever comes first. The landlord must take reasonable steps
to find one.

## 4. What the tenant may not do

4.1 No subletting of the whole property. One room may be let to a lodger with
the landlord's written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld.

4.2 No pets, except that this consent may not be unreasonably withheld for a
cat or a dog under 15kg where the tenant offers professional cleaning at the
end of the tenancy.

4.3 No alterations, including painting, without written consent. Picture hooks
and shelf brackets are not alterations.

## 5. Repairs

5.1 The landlord repairs the structure, exterior, heating, hot water and the
installations for gas, electricity and sanitation.

5.2 The tenant reports a defect within a reasonable time of noticing it. A
defect that worsens because it went unreported may be charged to the tenant to
the extent of the worsening, and no further.

5.3 An emergency repair — no heat, no water, a leak that cannot be contained —
is attended within 24 hours. Anything else is 14 days.
