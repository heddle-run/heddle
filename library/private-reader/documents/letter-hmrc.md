## Payments on account

A payment on account is an advance towards next year's bill. Each is half of
this year's tax and Class 4 National Insurance. You may apply to reduce them if
you expect next year's income to be lower — but if you reduce them below what
turns out to be due, we charge interest on the shortfall from the date each
payment was due.

## If you disagree

You have 30 days from the date of this letter to appeal. After that we can only
consider an appeal where you have a reasonable excuse for the delay; being
unaware of the deadline is not one.

## Interest and penalties

Interest runs from 1 February 2027 at the rate published on GOV.UK, currently
7.75%. A payment more than 30 days late also attracts a penalty of 5% of the
unpaid tax, repeated at 6 months and at 12 months.

## Expenses we have queried

Your return claims £1,880 of home-office expenses against self-employed income.
We are not opening an enquiry, but note that the simplified flat rate for 25 to
50 hours a month is £10 a month, and a claim above the flat rate needs records
apportioning the actual cost by floor area and by hours of business use. Keep
those records for five years after the 31 January filing deadline.
