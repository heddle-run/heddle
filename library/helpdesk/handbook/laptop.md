# Laptops

## Standard issue

Engineering gets a 16" MacBook Pro; everyone else gets a 14" MacBook Air. Both
arrive enrolled in Jamf with FileVault already on. Do not turn FileVault off —
the disk key is escrowed, and an unenrolled disk cannot be recovered by anyone
here.

## First-day setup

1. Sign in with the Google account IT sent to your personal address.
2. Let Jamf finish installing — it takes about twenty minutes and the machine
   will restart once on its own.
3. Install anything else from Self Service, which is in Applications.

## A broken or lost laptop

Report it the same day. A lost machine is wiped remotely within the hour, and a
machine that is merely broken is swapped from the spares cupboard on the third
floor — there is no loan pool beyond that cupboard, so a same-day swap depends
on somebody having returned one.

## Personal machines

Not permitted for anything that touches customer data. A personal machine can
read email and Slack through the browser and nothing else.
